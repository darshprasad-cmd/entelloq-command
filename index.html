Founder

The person behind the network

Darsh Prasad

Founder — Entelloq Networks

It started in a physics classroom. Darsh noticed that the students who struggled weren’t the least intelligent — more often, they were the ones who had quietly stopped believing they could understand. He saw the same pattern again in financial markets, where beginners face fragmented resources with no clear thread between concepts.

Entelloq Networks is his answer: an environment rather than a tool — AI-powered worlds for physics, markets and biology where you can manipulate the thing you’re learning, watch it respond, and feel understanding arrive. He designs, engineers and ships every product end-to-end.

“Understanding shouldn’t depend on where someone starts. If someone is willing to learn, they should have the opportunity to genuinely understand — regardless of the school they attend, the resources they have, or the opportunities they were born into.”

About

Mission

Intelligence. Innovation. Impact. Take people from “I don’t understand this” to “I finally get it” — by pairing rigorous content with AI that restores confidence instead of replacing effort.

Vision

An environment, not a tool: one connected platform of specialized worlds — physics you can touch, markets you can practise in safely, life you can explore — where every discipline gets a world-class product and every product is one click from the next.

What Entelloq means

EN — intelligent communication. TEL — expanding learning. LOQ — expanding options in trading, with quantitative precision. The name is the mission, spoken aloud: en-TELL-ock.
