Founder

Darsh PrasadFounder, Entelloq Networks
GitHubdarshprasad-cmd Emaildarshprasad@gmail.com Press featureSouth Asian Herald

Darsh Prasad founded Entelloq Networks after observing, first in physics classrooms and later in financial markets, that the people who struggle to learn are rarely the least capable — more often, they are the ones who have quietly stopped believing they could understand. Existing platforms address isolated stages of learning; Entelloq is built for what happens between them.

He designs and engineers every Entelloq product end-to-end — the physics simulation engine, the market-practice environment and the biology atlas — as one connected platform rather than a collection of separate tools.

“Technology achieves its highest purpose not when it thinks for people, but when it gives people the confidence to think for themselves.” Darsh Prasad — South Asian Herald, July 2026

About Entelloq Networks

Entelloq — pronounced en-TELL-ock.

Mission

Intelligence. Innovation. Impact. To take learners from “I don’t understand this” to “I finally get it” — by pairing rigorous content with AI that restores confidence rather than replacing effort.

Vision

An environment rather than a tool: a connected platform of specialised learning worlds, where every discipline receives a complete product and understanding does not depend on where someone starts.

The name

EN — intelligent communication. TEL — expanding learning. LOQ — expanding options in trading, with quantitative precision. The name carries the mission: en-TELL-ock.
